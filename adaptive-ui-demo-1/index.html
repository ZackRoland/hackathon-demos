Hackathon Project Notes

Teams are experimenting with browser-based assistants that can turn dense documentation into guided outputs. Judges care about clear user value, execution feasibility, and demo quality.

Current pain points include information overload, fragmented context across tabs, and poor task follow-through after meetings. A successful concept should reduce reading time and improve next-step clarity.

Potential risks include inaccurate summaries, weak personalization, and privacy concerns around tab data. Teams that show transparent controls and editable summaries are scoring higher in practice rounds.
